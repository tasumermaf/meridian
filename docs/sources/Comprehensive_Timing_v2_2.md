## A. Divine Hours (8-hour system)

### Foundational derivation

The Book of Three Responses establishes the eight-hour divine day through geometry: *"THEY ARE AS TWICE THE SIDES OF THE ETERNAL HOUSES OF THE KINGS, THE HOURS OF THE SKY AND OF THE FIRST STAR (= 8)."* The "Eternal Houses of the Kings" — royal tombs — have four sides. **Twice four equals eight**. Each divine day contains eight hours of sky-time.

### Hour calculation method (unequal hours)

Hours are calculated using the traditional unequal hours system: divide the actual daylight period into four equal parts (First Wing); divide the actual nighttime period into four equal parts (Second Wing). A **summer daylight divine hour is longer than a winter daylight divine hour**. This is essential for accurate Astrolabe implementation.

## B. Wing polarities (First Wing / Second Wing)

### Definition and timing

- **First Wing (Day)**: Spans from sunrise to sunset — Hours I through IV
- **Second Wing (Night)**: Spans from sunset to sunrise — Hours V through VIII

The Book states: *"SO MOVE THE WINGS OF GOD. THE WIND IS CONTINUOUS, EVERY FOUR TIMES 4 HOURS-SKY OF GOD."* Each 8-hour divine day divides into two 4-hour segments — the "wings" of the divine bird alternating.

### Wing transition timing

Wing transition occurs precisely at:
- **Sunrise**: Second Wing → First Wing
- **Sunset**: First Wing → Second Wing

These are **unequal hours calculations**, meaning the exact moment shifts seasonally with actual dawn/dusk times.

### "Wing tip touching peak" stellar geometry

Hour III is described as **"wing touches tail"** — this appears to reference the midday stellar geometry when the First Wing reaches its peak before declining toward Hour IV. Aura perception specifically favors this moment. This may indicate optimal stellar configuration for practices requiring maximum First Wing energy before the declining phase begins.

### Operations favored by each Wing

The documents provide limited explicit Wing-specific operation assignments beyond:
- **First Wing**: Generally associated with daylight operations, vision work, consecration
- **Second Wing**: Generally associated with night operations

**Note for Astrolabium v2.2**: The Wing layer may need expansion. The source documents suggest Wing polarity as foundational but provide fewer explicit operation-to-Wing mappings than expected.

### Projective/receptive polarity correlation

The Appendix A establishes that the Astrolabe's temporal layers correspond to the three bodies:
- **Soul body**: Primeval rhythm (~3.7 days lunar cycle)
- **Astral body**: Daily circulation (2-hour pathways)
- **Gross body**: Constant (Adonaj-Ba centers)

The Wing polarity (Day/Night) appears to modulate the astral circulation layer, suggesting:
- **First Wing**: More projective/emissive operations
- **Second Wing**: More receptive/receiving operations
