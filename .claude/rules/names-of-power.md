## What "Tier 0" means

Tier 0 is the highest evidence grade in the Falco isopsephy
methodology — a correspondence that's cross-tradition definitive
(verified isopsephic identity with a Greek theological term, plus
thematic coherence, plus structural placement that confirms intent).
Each of the thirteen Divine Month names has one or more Tier 0
interpretive readings. These are the readings that the Astrolabium
treats as definitive for the *character* of the month.

The isopsephic computation, prime-factor analysis, and Greek
correspondence work that established these readings is documented in
Appendix B v3 of the principal investigator's research (different
repository — `tasumermaf/`-adjacent Falco research environment).
**The Astrolabium does not redo that work.** It receives the Tier 0
character as a stable input and uses it to inform calendar-layer
readings.

## The Thirteen Names

### Month 1 — ISIS

**The Gatherer.** Exact Greek identity: **Ἶσις** — Isis, the Egyptian
goddess who gathered the dismembered Osiris. The first month of the
Divine Year, beginning at the Autumn Equinox. **Element: Air.** **Great
Rite: Autumn Equinox.**

The act of gathering — collecting the dispersed pieces of the past
year, the harvest gathered into the barn, the work of the upcoming
Nigredo movement. The month that initiates the Divine Year by drawing
together what was scattered.

### Month 2 — SADAM

**The Mirror-Operator.** SADAM = FOOR ("Noon"), the shadowless mirror
— the moment when the sun is directly overhead and no shadow falls.
The mirror operates *through* this shadowless point. **Element: Earth.**
**Great Rite: Day of the Dead.**

SADAM is the month of reflection without distortion. The Day of the
Dead falls in this month because the dead, who cast no shadow, are
most accessible through the mirror that knows no shadow.

### Month 3 — LIOTHIL

**The Helper.** Aids sacred writing
and transmission. The third month, when the year's gathering becomes
articulated and ready to be passed on. The scribe-month.

### Month 4 — EOROS

**The Director (Horus).** The Falcon-Headed presiding consciousness.
EOROS = HORUS = ΕΩΡΟΣ at omega-variant 445; in Protocol B,
ΕΩΡΟΣ = Κεντώ = "I weave." **Element: Fire.** **Great Rite: Winter
Solstice.**

The month in which the Falcon presides — the directing consciousness
of the developing year. Falco Tarassaco himself took the name *Falco*
in this lineage. The Winter Solstice falls in EOROS, the moment when
the dying sun is reborn — the Horus principle made calendrical.

### Month 5 — TASUMER

**The Builder.** TAS = SAT reversed = "to build, to make." TASUMER is
the month of construction — the work that begins after the Winter
Solstice's rebirth has occurred and the year begins building toward
the spring. The Albedo movement begins here.

Note: TASUMER MAF (the consulting practice's name) takes its name from
this same root — *the builder who will speak* (MAF = future tense of
*to speak*).

### Month 6 — MENON

**The Enduring One.** The silence-month.
The month of patience, of carrying the build forward through
silence and persistence. The month of disciplined continuation.

### Month 7 — OSIRIS

**The Resurrected.** Exact Greek identity: **Ὄσιρις**. Also τὸ ὅλον =
"The Whole." **Element: Ether.** **Great Rite: Spring Equinox.**

The month of resurrection — what was gathered by ISIS at the Autumn
Equinox and refined through Nigredo and the start of Albedo now
returns whole. The Spring Equinox falls in OSIRIS. The Whole is
restored.

### Month 8 — AGAFEST

**The Celebrant.** Contains SET (the destroyer name). Protocol B
reading: ISIS + OSIRIS reunited. The festival month — what was
gathered, resurrected, and made whole is now celebrated. The end of
Albedo; the threshold of Rubedo.

### Month 9 — SAMMA

**The Container (Rhombus).** The Tappetino
Proof month. The month in which the geometric container is itself
named. **Great Rite: Divine Marriage (May 24).**

The Divine Marriage rite — the marriage of Oberto (Falco Tarassaco)
to Damanhur, the wedding of the practitioner to the cosmological
container — falls in this month, on May 24. SAMMA *is* the container
that the marriage occurs within: the rhombic-dodecahedron tessitura
cocoon.

### Month 10 — SET

**The Destroyer.**
**Element: Water.** **Great Rite: Summer Solstice.**

The month of dissolution. The Summer Solstice falls in SET — the
moment when the sun reaches its highest point and begins its descent.
The peak that is also the beginning of decline. SET destroys not in
the sense of harm but in the sense of *bringing to an end so the new
cycle can begin*.

### Month 11 — SADAS

**The Mirror-Keeper.** Contains SADA = "Mirror" (a [SOURCE: Damanhurian]
dictionary entry). SADAS is palindromic and invariant. The month that
maintains the mirror through the post-solstice descent. The Rubedo
movement deepens.

### Month 12 — DESURIORIS

**The Sovereign.** SURI = "Tongue / Language." ORI = "Form." The month
of sovereign articulation — speaking in form, ruling through language.
The completion of the Rubedo arc; the last month of the regular
twelve.

### Month 13 — VADUSFADAHM (intercalary)

**The Activation.** The activation formula of
the entire Sacred Language.



The intercalary month inserts the conscience-power suffix between the
twelfth month and the start of the next year. It is the month of
**reflective activation** — the pause where awareness consolidates
the year's work before the next Autumn Equinox begins the next
gathering. Not present every year; intercalated when the lunar count
demands it (approximately 36.6% of years).

## Operational use in the Astrolabium

When the engine reports the current Divine Month, it includes the
Tier 0 character. A user looking at their state for May 25, 2026 will
see something like:

```
Divine Month: 9 — SAMMA (The Container / Rhombus)
              Great Rite proximity: Divine Marriage (May 24, yesterday)
              Movement: Rubedo (uniting)
```

The Tier 0 character is the *quality* that the month is bringing to
every other layer the practitioner reads. A Heart-window organ
reading during SAMMA carries the SAMMA-quality of containment and
geometric coherence; a Lung-window reading during SET carries the
SET-quality of destructive completion.

The instrument does not tell the practitioner what to feel. It tells
them which quality is dressing the moment, so the practitioner can
recognize what kind of moment this is.

## What never to claim

- Never claim a Tier 0 reading is just analytical opinion. The
  isopsephic verification work was rigorous and adversarial; the
  readings have been audited. They are interpretive in nature but
  established within the methodology.
- Never claim the month-name isopsephic values drive the Astrolabium's
  clock. They do not. The clock is solar position, lunar phase, and
  stem-branch arithmetic. The names carry the *qualitative character*
  of each month; the *clock arithmetic* is independent.
- Never substitute a different reading for a Name of Power. The Tier
  0 readings are what they are. If a user proposes a different reading,
  acknowledge their proposal, mark it as their proposal, and explain
  why the Tier 0 reading was selected.
