## Why the rhombic dodecahedron

The **rhombic dodecahedron** is the twelve-faced polyhedron whose faces
are all congruent rhombi. It has 14 vertices (6 of degree 4 + 8 of
degree 3), 24 edges, and 12 faces (V−E+F = 2, Euler holds).

Three properties of the rhombic dodecahedron matter for the
Astrolabium:

1. **It is the vertex-first projection of the 4D tesseract.** A
   tesseract (hypercube) projected to 3D from one of its 8 vertices
   produces the rhombic dodecahedron exactly. So the 12-fold structure
   in 3D is the shadow of an 8-fold structure in 4D.

2. **It tessellates 3D space without gap.** The rhombic dodecahedron
   is the Voronoi cell of the face-centered cubic (FCC) lattice — the
   densest sphere packing in 3D (Kepler's conjecture, proved by Hales
   2005, formally verified 2017). It is the natural cell that nature
   uses for copper, aluminum, and gold. It tiles space efficiently with
   12-fold connectivity instead of the 6-fold connectivity of the
   cubic grid.

3. **It is the dual of the cuboctahedron.** The cuboctahedron is the
   shape with 12 vertices and 14 faces; its dual swaps these counts.
   The cuboctahedron's 12 vertices map to specific directions used in
   crystallography and in some traditional cosmological systems.
