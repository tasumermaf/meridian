## The geometry (standard mathematics)

The rhombic dodecahedron has 12 congruent rhombic faces, 24 edges, and
14 vertices (6 of degree 4 + 8 of degree 3). Three standard properties:

1. **It is the vertex-first projection of the 4D tesseract** — the
   hypercube's 3D shadow from one of its vertices.
2. **It tessellates 3D space without gap** — it is the Voronoi cell of
   the face-centered cubic lattice, the densest sphere packing in 3D
   (Kepler's conjecture; Hales 2005, formally verified 2017).
3. **It is the dual of the cuboctahedron.**
