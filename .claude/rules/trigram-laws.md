## The architectural statement

The eight Primeval Laws of Damanhurian cosmology map to the eight
trigrams of the *I Ching* in a specific way:

- **Six** of the eight trigrams (Kūn, Zhèn, Duì, Qián, Xùn, Gèn) carry
  six cyclic Laws through the lunar month, each trigram presiding over
  one ~4.9-day lunar phase.
- **Two** of the eight trigrams (Kǎn and Lí) are *not* in the lunar
  cycle. They are the **Solar Keys** — permanent Laws that activate at
  sunrise (Kǎn ☵ / Gold Key) and sunset (Lí ☲ / Silver Key).

This is the **6+2 architecture.** It is the central design statement of
the Astrolabium's Soul + Keys layering.

## Where the 6+2 split comes from

The split is not invented for the Astrolabium. It is independently
attested in **three traditions**:

### 1. The Cantong qi (參同契)

The *Cantong qi* (the "Tripartite Equation" or "Token of the Three") is
the foundational Daoist alchemical text, attributed to Wèi Bóyáng (c.
142 CE). It describes the lunar month as a sequence of trigram
configurations that govern the alchemical fire phasing (火候 *huǒ
hòu*). The Cantong qi explicitly **excludes Kǎn and Lí from the lunar
cycle** and treats them as the operators of the alchemical work itself
— Kǎn (water/lead) and Lí (fire/mercury) are the substances being
worked, not the phases of the work. The six remaining trigrams cycle
through the month.

[SOURCE: TCM — Cantong qi, classical Daoist alchemy]

### 2. The Plum Blossom Fist Form (梅花拳)

The Plum Blossom martial / qi-cultivation system from Wǔ Méi Kung Fu
(Sifu Ken Lo lineage, NYC, 7th generation transmission claimed) trains
movement on the Posterior Heaven Bagua. The fundamental fist form
**avoids the Water positions** (Kǎn ☵ and Lí ☲) when traversing the
trigram pattern. Both Key trigrams carry the **Water** correspondence
in the Plum Blossom elemental system. Water is treated as *the medium
of transit*, not part of the cycle.

[SOURCE: TCM / Wu Mei Kung Fu transmission. Independently confirmed
March 2, 2026 — see `docs/sources/plum_blossom_venus_rose_note.md`]

### 3. The Book of Three Responses (mirror transit)

The Damanhurian *Book of Three Responses* describes the practitioner's
mirror-transit work and identifies Water as the medium of the transit
itself. The mirror is Water; the practitioner moves *through* Water;
Water is therefore *outside the cycle of operations being performed*.
The same trigram split — six trigrams for the operations, two
(both Water-bearing) for the medium — appears independently in the
Damanhurian system.

[SOURCE: Damanhurian — Book of Three Responses]

**Three independent traditions converge on the same 6+2 split.** This
convergence is one of the analytical contributions that confirmed the
Astrolabium architecture, and it was the trigger for locking the spec
on March 1, 2026.

## The Six Cyclic Laws

| Lunar phase                       | Trigram   | Pinyin  | Law                     |
|-----------------------------------|-----------|---------|-------------------------|
| New Moon (0° elongation)          | Kūn ☷    | Earth   | **Kaos**                |
| Waxing Crescent                   | Zhèn ☳   | Thunder | **Sole Atom**           |
| First Quarter / Waxing Gibbous    | Duì ☱    | Lake    | **Time Matrix**         |

Each phase lasts approximately **4.926 days** (the synodic month is
29.5306 days, divided by 6).

### The Laws, one at a time

**Kaos** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. "Dimensions without Substance" — the
formless ground from which form emerges. New Moon, maximum
receptivity, Kūn ☷ (pure yin, Earth). The reservoir of potential.
[Prime correspondence: 23.]

**Sole Atom** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The defining particle — the single
indivisible seed of continuity. Waxing Crescent, the moon first
visible. Zhèn ☳ (Thunder, the eldest son, first yang appearing). The
first shock: the defining particle breaks through the formless ground.
[Prime correspondence: 19.]

**Time Matrix** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The structure within which
sequence becomes possible. First Quarter through Waxing Gibbous. Duì
☱ (Lake, the youngest daughter, joy). The rhythm that sequences
events. [Prime correspondence: 29.]

**Synchronicity** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The active selection of
coherence — which events cohere into the same moment. Full Moon,
maximum illumination, Qián ☰ (pure yang, Heaven). The principle of
meaningful coincidence. [Prime correspondence: 89.]

**Geometric Essence** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The mathematical form that
underlies physical structure. Waning Gibbous. Xùn ☴ (Wind, the eldest
daughter, penetration). Pattern made visible. [Prime correspondence: 67.]

**Arrow of Complexity** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The real direction of the
universe — complexity as the capacity to carry value forward. Last Quarter
through Waning Crescent. Gèn ☶ (Mountain, the youngest son, stillness).
The threshold: mass decreasing, complexity increasing — the thread that
points past the cycle's end. [Prime correspondence: 17.]

## The Two Permanent Laws (Solar Keys)

| Key            | Trigram   | Element   | Permanent Law           | Cusp        |
|----------------|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|-------------|
| **Gold Key**   | Kǎn ☵    | Water     | **Fall of Events**      | Sunrise     |
| **Silver Key** | Lí ☲     | Fire/Water (in Plum Blossom) | **Divinity** | Sunset      |

(Lí is classically Fire in standard I Ching; in the Plum Blossom
elemental system used here, Lí also carries Water as the *medium* —
this is what makes both Keys "Water-bearing" in the 6+2 split.)

**Fall of Events** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The principle by which events
arrive at the practitioner — the descending current of incident from
the field of all possibilities into a specific moment. Sunrise as the
threshold where the day's events begin to fall. [Prime correspondence:
11.]

**Divinity** [SOURCE: Damanhurian]. The principle of the divine
character — the unique essence that persists across all
transformations. Sunset as the threshold where the day's events return
to the field, leaving only the essence. [Prime correspondence: 31.]

The Keys are not periodic. They activate every day at sunrise and
sunset, briefly, during the **cusping window** that brackets the cusp
itself. The cusping window is the civil twilight duration at the
practitioner's latitude — at Damanhur (45°N), ~25–35 minutes; near the
equator, shorter; at high latitudes, much longer.

## Why this matters operationally

The 6+2 split has a direct operational consequence: **Solar Keys can
activate during any Primeval Law phase**, not just specific ones. A
practitioner doing work at sunrise during a Full Moon receives the
Silver Key the same day they receive the Full Moon's Synchronicity —
two separate layers, two separate qualities. The engine reports both,
plus the compound (Key-Amplified Law Unity) when one of the cyclic
six matches the Derivative Law during cusping.

This is also why the Cantong qi treats sunrise and sunset as ritually
weightiest: those are the moments when the alchemical fire is *most
precisely controllable* because two layers (the Permanent Law and
whatever Cyclic Law is currently active) are both available, and a
compound is possible.
