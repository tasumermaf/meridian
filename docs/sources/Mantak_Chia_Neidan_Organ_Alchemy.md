# Mantak Chia's neidan organ alchemy: a complete correspondence system

Mantak Chia's Universal Healing Tao system provides one of the most systematized Western presentations of Taoist internal alchemy, mapping emotional energies to organs, elements, trigrams, and extraordinary vessels in an interconnected framework designed for "compound alignment." The core principle is transformational: negative emotions stored in organs are neither suppressed nor expressed but collected, purified in an alchemical "cauldron," crystallized into a mobile energy "pearl," and circulated to cultivate positive virtues. This report extracts the complete correspondences from Chia's major works for use in mapping to the Eight Trigrams and Eight Extraordinary Vessels.

## Six Healing Sounds complete practice

The Six Healing Sounds (Liu Zi Jue) release excess heat and negative emotions through specific vibrations. Chia recommends the sequence **Lungs → Kidneys → Liver → Heart → Spleen → Triple Warmer**, performed 3-6 repetitions each, sub-vocally (felt internally, barely audible).

### Complete sound methodology

**1. Lung Sound (SSSSSSS)** — Metal, Autumn
- *Posture*: Raise arms overhead, palms up pushing outward, elbows rounded
- *Emotion release*: Sadness, grief → Courage, righteousness
- *Visualization*: White light filling lungs like fresh mountain air
- *Energy direction*: Releases excess heat; draws in cool white metal energy

**2. Kidney Sound (WOOOOO)** — Water, Winter
- *Posture*: Feet together, clasp hands around knees, round back, look up
- *Emotion release*: Fear → Gentleness, stillness, alertness
- *Visualization*: Bright blue light filling kidneys
- *Energy direction*: Compresses kidney sacs, expels fear energy downward

**3. Liver Sound (SHHHHH)** — Wood, Spring
- *Posture*: Arms overhead with interlocked fingers, lean LEFT to stretch right side
- *Emotion release*: Anger, frustration → Kindness, generosity
- *Visualization*: Green light surrounding and penetrating liver
- *Energy direction*: Clears heat from liver; energy rises and expands

**4. Heart Sound (HAWWWW)** — Fire, Summer
- *Posture*: Arms overhead with interlocked fingers, lean RIGHT to stretch left side
- *Emotion release*: Hatred, impatience → Love, joy, happiness
- *Visualization*: Red light burning away cruelty and self-pity
- *Energy direction*: Releases "heartfire" through esophagus

**5. Spleen Sound (WHOOOO)** — Earth, Late Summer
- *Posture*: Hands under left ribcage, push up and in while exhaling
- *Emotion release*: Worry, anxiety → Fairness, trust, groundedness
- *Visualization*: Golden yellow light entering spleen, stomach, pancreas
- *Energy direction*: Regulates body temperature; balances digestive fire

**6. Triple Warmer Sound (HEEEEE)** — All Elements, All Seasons
- *Posture*: Lying flat on back
- *Function*: Balances the three burning spaces (upper/hot, middle/warm, lower/cool)
- *Emotion release*: Panic, imbalance → Peace, harmony
- *Energy direction*: Pushes heat downward from head to feet like a rolling pin

### The pearl concept

The **pearl** (内丹, nèi dān) is a crystallized sphere of purified life-force energy, central to Fusion practice:

- **Formation location**: Lower Tan Tien "cauldron" — the space between navel and Ming Men, approximately 3 inches in diameter, positioned toward the back in front of the kidneys
- **Creation process**: Five organ energies are drawn through pakuas to collection points, mixed and blended in the cauldron, where vibrating essences of the Eight Creative Forces neutralize negativity and condense the energy into a luminous pearl
- **Circulation**: Guided by Yi (intention) and mind/eye power, the pearl moves through orbit points, absorbing Universal and Earth Forces while cleansing organs and glands
- **Relationship to consciousness**: The pearl represents "transferred consciousness" — the initial phase of projecting awareness to a new realm; it becomes the seed for the "energy body" or "soul body" in advanced practices

## Fusion practices and pakua arrangements

Fusion of Five Elements employs **four pakuas** (八卦, bagua) positioned around the body as "eight-sided energy transformers."

### The four pakua positions

1. **Front Pakua**: 1.5" inward from navel
2. **Back Pakua**: 1.5" inward from Ming Men (Door of Life)
3. **Left Pakua**: At intersection of left armpit line and navel level, 1.5" inward
4. **Right Pakua**: At intersection of right armpit line and navel level, 1.5" inward

The front/back pakuas function as one pair; the left/right pakuas as another. Both pairs spiral, draw, refine, and condense energy into the central cauldron.

### Later Heaven trigram-organ mappings

Chia uses the **Later Heaven (King Wen) Bagua** arrangement:

| Trigram | Symbol | Name | Direction | Element | Organ | Emotion |
|---------|--------|------|-----------|---------|-------|---------|
| **Li** | ☲ | Fire | South | Fire | Heart | Hate → Love |
| **Kun** | ☷ | Earth | Southwest | Earth | Spleen | Worry → Trust |
| **Dui** | ☱ | Lake | West | Metal | Lungs | Grief → Courage |
| **Qian** | ☰ | Heaven | Northwest | Metal | Lungs (yang) | Strength |
| **Kan** | ☵ | Water | North | Water | Kidneys | Fear → Gentleness |
| **Gen** | ☶ | Mountain | Northeast | Earth | Spleen (yang) | Stillness |
| **Zhen** | ☳ | Thunder | East | Wood | Liver | Anger → Kindness |
| **Xun** | ☴ | Wind | Southeast | Wood | Liver (yin) | Flexibility |

The eight trigrams consolidate to five elements: **Kan** (Water/Kidneys), **Li** (Fire/Heart), **Kun + Gen** (Earth/Spleen), **Zhen + Xun** (Wood/Liver), **Qian + Dui** (Metal/Lungs).

### Kan and Li alchemy

The **Kan ☵ (Water)** and **Li ☲ (Fire)** relationship is central to advanced practices:

- **Kan**: Kidneys, yin essence with yang within (single yang line between two yin), "fire within water," sexual/reproductive energy, cold
- **Li**: Heart, yang nature with yin within (single yin line between two yang), "water within fire," compassion energy, hot

Kan & Li practice **reverses the natural arrangement** (fire normally rises, water sinks) to create alchemical "steaming" that transforms jing (sexual essence) into chi (vitality).

Energy fills these vessels in sequence: When the Lower Tan Tien is full, it overflows into Du and Ren (Microcosmic Orbit), then into the remaining six extraordinary vessels, and finally into the 12 regular organ meridians.

### Governor Vessel (Du Mai) spiritual qualities

Called the **"Sea of Yang"**, Du Mai governs all yang meridians and carries ascending energy up the spine:

- **Willpower and purpose**: Determination, creativity, genius
- **Divine connection**: Link to heavenly/cosmic forces  
- **Vitality**: Physical and spiritual strength
- **Sympathetic activation**: Fight-or-flight response
- **Consciousness**: Governs brain, spinal cord, central nervous system

Key points: Wei Lu (tailbone gate), Ming Men (willpower/prenatal essence), Da Zhui (all yang channels meet), Bai Hui (heaven connection)

### Conception Vessel (Ren Mai) emotional qualities

Called the **"Sea of Yin"**, Ren Mai governs all yin meridians and carries descending energy down the front:

- **Nurturing and bonding**: Capacity to receive love and nurture
- **Self-love**: Foundation for emotional completeness
- **Receptivity**: Ability to absorb, nourish, bond
- **Parasympathetic activation**: Rest-and-digest response
- **Reproductive function**: Conception, pregnancy, birthing

**Balanced**: Emotional warmth, deep connection, vulnerability, feeling complete
**Imbalanced**: Fear of closeness, emotional rigidity, difficulty trusting, searching externally for completion

Key points: Hui Yin (gate to Du Mai), Qihai (Sea of Qi), Shenque (navel/maternal memory), Shanzhong (chest emotions)
