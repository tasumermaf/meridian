## Synchronicity

Synchronicity is the law through which events, Form and its continuous transformation on the axis of the temporal flow, are selected by virtue of the direction conferred on the entire system: the evolutionary direction of the universe.

Synchronicity represents the exasperation, from our point of view of the laws of cause and effect, expanding the field of the possible into infinite time, where in fact each probable event becomes certain. The selection of each event is realized through criteria which go beyond the individual, conforming to a movement that is as extensive as the entire universe. To enter into the use of these laws signifies to go along with the evolution of humanity but not only this.

The Synchronic Lines, the roads of life, represent one of the functional aspects of this law through which synchronicity, under certain aspects, can manifest itself other than in the universal nervous system.

From the philosophical point of view, but also simply para-psychological, synchronicity offers answers to many questions; for example, so called miracles, or telekinetic phenomena, by maintaining every event as realizable if it is inserted into the right temporal and synchronic combination. In fact miracles in themselves represent an absurd exception to the laws of the universe, when these laws can be excellently applied to manifest phenomena that apparently contradict the (known) laws of Physics.

The term 'paranormal' is profoundly imprecise but in order to correct it we would have to correct the accepted idea of the term 'normal', widening it to include all that is proposed by Esoteric Physics.

Synchronicity is a law that leaves aside the commonly considered sequence of cause and effect and acts by respondence.

## Sole Atom

The sole atom is that infinitesimal particle, which, at zero time, (infinite speed) travels along the line of symmetry in every Form defining it inside the universe.

It is the law that allows every Form to renew itself continually and contemporaneously, the armistice of laws that affiliates Form to this universe.

## Divinity

This is the law through which a divine presence can participate in the world of Form. Such presence as laws is not determined by its qualification, therefore we cannot say that we are speaking of soul, Divine Spark, the observer's presence or of a fragment of the 'mirror' that is more or less aware and sentient. We are limited to saying that it represents a divine presence in every Form of the universe that is more or less able to reach any kind of consciousness of self or capacity to perceive. Obviously in complex Form this expresses itself in such a way as to produce an active participation inside an intelligent structure, until it arrives at sustaining an aware experience in a human soul structure.

Naturally the law of divinity refers to the Primeval Divinity Humankind, which expresses its evolutionary immanence in Form.

If the sole atom designs Form, then geometric essence is its ruler!

This law represents the mapping into which Form is placed within the fabric of time and space; it can be said that it defines the territory, the 'landscape' that all Form can re-enter on the basis of a precise measurement. This is why the concept of geometry is made reference to, even though we need to extend the significance of the term.

## Time Matrix

The universe proceeds at a constant and at the same time dynamic equilibrium. Every variation, transformation of mass inside Form (of mass and energy and vice versa) has to find the right compensation so that the total mass equilibrium can maintain itself. On the other hand this does not mean that such compensation must uniformly involve the entire universe, only that Form that 'responds' with representative Form.

And here we can apply one of the principles of Magic which is pronounced in the Ermete Trismegisto of the Emerald Tablet: 'Like responds to Like'. But what do we mean by 'like'? Certainly it does not mean that Form is identical or that it has the same organic characteristics, or frequents the same territory or that it is close enough together. The similarity is based upon a relationship (we could say quantistic) of 'concordant temporality' to which Form with similar time responds.

As we have seen, time is the measure of the speed of the transformation/evolution of Form, its participation in a determined flow of complexity.

The law of the Time Matrix measures all time (nodes of complexity) of all Form; that is it creates the presuppositions of correspondence to maintain the system in equilibrium.

It is the law that puts all Form into relationship; it is the connector, the network of communication. It also functions as the law which maintains the relationship of respondence with other Form on the temporal plane of reference.

## The Fall (Cascade) of (Neutral) Events

What is an event?

Surely all Form is an event and it is for this reason that events lose the adjective 'neutral' at the moment in which they enter the armistice. In fact as Primeval Laws one talks of the 'Cascade of Neutral Events' because these 'events' do not have a predestined manifestation and are ready to fall in line with that which springs from an armistice. In our case therefore, events can do nothing else but manifest themselves in Form, ours being the universe of Form and as the nature of Derivative Laws envisages exactly this possibility, it is correct to say the 'Fall of Events'.

In any case, to talk of events simply as Form is rather restrictive; the word 'event' is linked to the concept of historical succession, of action, happenings and not so much to Form even though every action, every fact is translated into Form or into the transformation of Form.

In effect to define what is an 'event' depends upon the subject that is being considered: for a simple stone the event will be the stone itself, or the sand that will derive from the erosion of it. Little by little we arrive at ourselves as living Forms, intelligent and sentient. The concept of event is extended, no longer only referring to our intrinsic form but what happens to it and how much it is able to make things happen; it is the relationship between things, interpreted as history.

Probably a divinity would have an even wider concept of events as the object of its 'coloration'.

If we talk of it as a 'spiritual ecosystem'; the various psycho-creatures, the territorial divinities, 'colour' events so that they can produce themselves on the territory over which they preside, in such a manner as to safeguard the equilibrium and above all the nourishment necessary for their maintenance and growth.

It is a sign, a brand that colours the event, which continues on its journey towards Form perhaps through other levels of entities before arriving at the object. The last of these territorial entities is Humankind, who with the use of Free Will and capacity to make choices uses it to 'saturate' the event, manifesting it.

This action can be translated into the various physical reactions in the transformation of Form and can in turn be a more or less powerful fuse for other sequences of possible saturation.

Thus in the space-time territory we can meet saturated events or even unsaturated events according to choice and the capacity of Humankind to use it.

The fall of events is not uniform even though it follows lines of major intensity: the Synchronic Lines for example. Where knots of lines exist, the fall of events can be so intense that it is probable that the formation of real mines of unsaturated events will be found, perhaps coloured by the divinity. These places are of particular importance and strategic for the application of real Magic operations, above all with regard to the technology employed in space-time travel.

## Chaos

Chaos is always a successive form of order.

It is a typical law needed to define the system as a whole. In effect it manifests itself to us in a reality already well defined, therefore it is difficult to imagine a situation of chaos. By this term we mean something different from the scientific concept of 'primordial soup'.

Nevertheless philosophy or religion comes close to this concept when it defines 'chaos' as something that is not yet defined by the attention of God which orders things through its action. The Word that distinguishes the elements, of the sky from the earth, that creates light! Let's define chaos as that combination of non-directed events without substance that are not even touched by the act of coloration on the part of the divinity.

Please note however.

From a specifically esoteric point of view, Chaos represents an occasion for superseding the existing order to reach a superior level, the disorder that anticipates access to a more perfect and elegant model of reality.

It is the capacity to put equilibrium into discussion, to find a new one, of transforming reality or one's own image of reality in favour of wider horizons. Chaos is an evolutionary element, physical and philosophical, through which continuous change is realized; the demolition of fixed and unchangeable principles, the transformation, the attainment of a new 'level of justice', a stable evolutionary point.

Chaos is a subjective and limited perception of a superior order. Being a law, which signifies that the universe of Form cannot 'result' in a single definitive and absolute truth.

## Arrow of Complexity

Our universe is Divinity Humankind's opportunity for expansion, in which it will have to meet the experiential result of all the Form outside of Form itself, so that the mythical 'mirror' can recompose itself. In fact few know about the nature of this expansion, of the motivation and the dynamics which render it necessary. We must reason with the mind of a Primeval Divinity to access such a level of awareness.

On the other hand it is here in this space-time that the expansion must be realized, therefore it is legitimate to ask ourselves: "...but Form, what does it have to do to realize this expansion? What has to happen so that goals are reached that transcend Form itself? How can any Form satisfy the realization of a result which enables it to exit from the system and carry itself into the absolute...?"

The Derivative Law of the 'Arrow of Complexity' indicates direction. This is truly the real direction of the universe, over and beyond any considered element of space or time.

By 'complexity' we mean the capacity of each Form to express a value, significance. It is as if each Form in the universe contains a function, a specific working capacity, information that represents its role and at the same time 'memory', meant as experiential value, significance.

The arrow of complexity represents the 'cone' that angle of maximum functionality optimized within Form that expresses a transcendent added value.

Let's look at it from another perspective.

Each Form must be able to represent a function co-ordinated with the whole system which is becoming forever more comprehensive and more optimized. It is as if working capacity or information is forever increasing supported by a referential mass that is forever reducing, whilst at the same time becoming more 'organized' internally.

The mass is in inverse proportion to the complexity (level of organization) up until a critical point in which all the information no longer needs mass to sustain itself.

The capacity to formulate an added value which is able to exit from the reference of mass is called 'complexity'. Form made of mass, which is forever reducing, will be able to sustain forever more extensive information-function up until the point that it leaves the system of mass itself (the universe) with this particular result. We find ourselves confronted with something that represents Form and which is a direct emanation, but is not located in Form.

This turns out to contain all the significance, the value of the Form, without the Form having to be the bearer of it. With the result that it is then conducted outside the universe, where it will constitute nourishment for the Primeval Divinity.

This phenomenon is constantly being produced and represents the maintenance element of the universe of Form as an expression of existent being.

It is easy to understand the significance of complexity when for example we think of Art. In effect the emotions, the feelings, the profound significance that we give to a work of Art, are not located inside the elements that produce it (canvas and colour if we observe a painting) nor inside a precise section of the brain of its creator. Neither is it measurable through an investigation of what happens inside our bodies.

It is an emotional result that produces an interior enrichment able to influence behaviour and choice (events) but which is given off by every Form we like to consider. It is added value. It is complexity.

Complexity is gradually derived from Form but it is incomplete, it is stratified in a particular environment that we have learnt to define as the 'Threshold', a particular state of being in which information, value and thought do not need to have Form to be maintained. It is a field of Primeval Laws (no Form therefore no armistice) that we place between the universe of Form and the Real, an absolute and definitive state in which the complete result is reached. Complexity is produced as a flow and must be sustained and enriched until it reaches a particular 'point of no return', levels of realization in which different orders of functionality are created. These levels have come to be defined as 'levels of justice'.

The significance is in proportion to our capacity to perceive and interpret: it is the awareness that gives preciousness to Form.

We are not talking about simple progress but of an acquired value that re-enters the field of spirituality, as a capacity to give significance to things, which goes beyond the things themselves bringing us back to eternal values.

The diverse complexity of a Form also represents the flow of time within which the Form is maintained. It is possible to say that Form which is more complex travels at a faster time in respect to less complex Form; this is why the evolutionary result (work) is different even though it has been realized in equal amounts of time.

The arrow of complexity indicates the direction of the exit of our universe and also represents a vehicle through which we move ourselves into different states of being.
