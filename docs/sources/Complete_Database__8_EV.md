## Critical intersection points for timing systems

For Ling Gui Ba Fa implementation, certain points carry special significance due to their multi-vessel or multi-channel convergence:

**BL-1 Jingming** represents the most clinically important convergence point—where Yin Qiao Mai and Yang Qiao Mai meet at the inner canthus, regulating the opening and closing of the eyes and sleep-wake cycles.

**GV-14 Dazhui** serves as the meeting point for all six yang channels plus Du Mai, making it the supreme yang convergence point for timing calculations affecting yang qi.

**CV-3 and CV-4** are where Spleen, Liver, and Kidney channels converge on Ren Mai, creating the critical lower jiao intersection for reproductive and urogenital timing.

**CV-17 Shanzhong** represents the upper jiao convergence of four channels (SP, KI, SI, SJ) and serves as the Hui-meeting point of Qi and Front-Mu of Pericardium.
