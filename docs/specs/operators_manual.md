## What Is the Astrolabium?

The Astrolabium Caudae Rubrae — the Red Tail Astrolabe — is a temporal
navigation instrument. It answers one question: **"What is the
alchemical quality of this moment?"**

Not "what time is it" (your phone does that). Not "what should I do"
(that is your business). The Astrolabium tells you what the moment
*is made of* — which cosmic principles are active, which energetic
channels are open, which organs are peaking, which phase of the lunar
cycle is governing, and whether any of these layers happen to be saying
the same thing at the same time.

Think of it as a weather report for the soul. You do not control the
weather. But if you know a thunderstorm is coming, you might choose
to stay indoors — or you might choose to stand on the hill with your
arms open. The Astrolabium gives you the forecast. What you do with
it is between you and the storm.

### What It Is Not

The Astrolabium does not warn, prohibit, or prescribe. It does not
tell you that a moment is "good" or "bad." It does not require you to
report your attainment level or spiritual status. The practitioner is
assumed to be perfected — the instrument simply displays what is
available. A master chef uses a thermometer; the thermometer does not
tell the chef how to cook.

### Where It Comes From

The Astrolabium synthesizes five ancient systems into a single unified
instrument:

1. **Ling Gui Ba Fa** (靈龜八法, "Sacred Turtle Eight Methods") —
   a Chinese chronoacupuncture system for determining which of the
   eight Extraordinary Vessels is currently open
2. **The Eight Primeval Laws** — Falco Tarassaco's cosmological
   principles governing the universe of Form
3. **The Organ Clock** — the Traditional Chinese Medicine system
   mapping organs to solar-position-based hours
4. **The Divine Hours** — an eight-fold division of the day and night
   from the Book of Three Responses
5. **The Lunisolar Calendar** — thirteen Divine Months, the Sephirotic
   Week, and the six Great Rites of the Damanhurian year

Each of these systems is centuries or millennia old. The Astrolabium's
original contribution is demonstrating that they share a common temporal
substrate — they all derive from the same astronomical foundations
(solar position and lunar phase) — and synthesizing them into a single
readable display.

## The Three Bodies

The Astrolabium reads three temporal bodies simultaneously. These are
not metaphors. They are distinct layers of temporal experience that
operate at different speeds:

### The Soul Layer — The Slow Rhythm (Lunar)

**What governs it:** The Moon's phase within the synodic month.

**What it tells you:** Which of the six cyclic Primeval Laws is active
right now. The Moon waxes and wanes through six phases, each carrying
a different Law:

| Phase | What You See | Law | Quality |
|-------|-------------|-----|---------|
| New Moon | Invisible | **Kaos** | The void before the Word. Disorder anticipating a superior order. The demolition of fixed principles |
| First Crescent | Thin waxing crescent | **Sole Atom** | The defining particle. The armistice that affiliates Form to this universe. Renewal |
| First Quarter | Half-lit, waxing | **Time Matrix** | The connector. The network that puts all Form into relationship. Communication |
| Full Moon | Fully lit | **Synchronicity** | The evolutionary direction of the universe. Maximum coherent force. Cause and effect expanded into infinite time |
| First Wane | Waning gibbous | **Geometric Essence** | The mapping of Form within the fabric of time and space. Territory. Memory |
| Last Quarter | Half-lit, waning | **Arrow of Complexity** | The exit direction of the universe. Mass decreasing, complexity increasing. The Threshold |

The cycle takes roughly 29.5 days and repeats. Each phase lasts about
4.9 days. You can track it by looking at the Moon.

### The Solar Keys — The Water Gates

**What governs them:** Sunrise and sunset.

**What they tell you:** Two cusping moments — brief windows where the
hinge between the two wings of the day opens. Day yields to night, night
to day. These are events, not periods.

| Event | Key | Trigram | Quality | When |
|-------|-----|---------|---------|------|
| Sunrise | **Gold Key** | Kǎn ☵ | **Fall of Events** | Morning civil twilight (~30 min) |
| Sunset | **Silver Key** | Lí ☲ | **Divinity** | Evening civil twilight (~30 min) |

The assignment follows Neidan reversal logic: at the moment yang rises,
work with the container of yang (Gold Key / Kǎn, true lead). At the
moment yin descends, work with the container of yin (Silver Key / Lí,
true mercury).

**Why "Water Gates."** In Plum Blossom Alchemy, both Key trigrams carry
Water — Kǎn is Yin Water (through the Kidney meridian), Lí is Yang
Water (through the Bladder meridian). Water is the medium of transit and
transformation. The Keys are not cyclic Laws — they are the gates through
which the cycle passes at the daily solar cusps.

The Cantong qi tradition excludes Kǎn and Lí from the lunar cycle —
they are the alchemical operators, not cyclic phases. The Plum Blossom
martial form independently confirms this: its flowing attack pattern
traces through six trigram positions on the Posterior Heaven Bagua and
avoids the two Water positions. Two traditions, different continents,
the same exclusion.

**The 6+2 Architecture.** Six trigrams carry six Laws through the lunar
month. Two trigrams (Kǎn and Lí) stand outside the cycle as the Water
gates, activating at the solar cusps daily, regardless of the Moon's
position. Fall of Events and Divinity are the qualities these gates
carry — permanent, daily, and structurally distinct from the six cyclic
Laws.

**What it tells you:** Which of the eight Extraordinary Vessels of

## The Organ Clock

**What governs it:** Solar position (the same 12-fold division used for
the Earthly Branches).

**What it tells you:** Which of the twelve primary meridians is most
active right now. The day and night are each divided into six unequal
parts, and each part corresponds to an organ:

**Day (sunrise → sunset):**
Stomach → Spleen → Heart → Small Intestine → Bladder → Kidney

**Night (sunset → sunrise):**
Pericardium → San Jiao → Gallbladder → Liver → Lung → Large Intestine

Each organ carries an emotional pair — a positive and negative pole.
The Heart window (around solar noon) carries Love/Joy (+) and
Hatred/Arrogance (−). The Kidney window (late afternoon) carries
Gentleness/Wisdom (+) and Fear/Paranoia (−). These are not
prescriptions. They are descriptions of what is energetically
available.

**Important:** These are unequal hours. In summer, the day windows are
longer. In winter, they are shorter. The Astrolabium always computes
from actual sunrise and sunset at your location — never from a clock.

## Plum Blossom Alchemy — The Bridge

The Astrolabium operates two elemental systems simultaneously: the
**8-trigram system** (carrying Primeval Laws through the lunar month)

Through the body.

Each of the eight Extraordinary Vessels has a **confluent point** — an
acupuncture point that opens the vessel. That point sits on a specific

every vessel, and by extension every trigram, inherits a Wu Xing element

The Fire meridians split into **Imperial Fire** (Small Intestine, the

The name comes from the **Wu Mei Kung Fu Plum Blossom** (梅花): five
petals (five elements) surrounding eight rays (eight trigrams). The
symbol IS the bridge — the five-fold elemental system wrapping the

## Law Unity — When the Layers Agree

Most of the time, the different temporal layers produce different Laws.
The Moon might say "Kaos" while the LGBF vessel says "Synchronicity."
This is normal. The Astrolabium displays both, and the practitioner
holds the tension.

But occasionally, two or more layers produce the **same Law at the same
time**. This is called **Law Unity**, and it is operatively significant:

### Two-Body Unity

The Soul layer (lunar phase) and the Astral layer (LGBF vessel) produce
the same Law simultaneously. Example: the Moon is in the Full Moon phase
(Synchronicity) AND the LGBF opens Du Mai (also Synchronicity). Both the
slow rhythm and the fast rhythm align. This occurs roughly 6.7% of all
moments — uncommon but not rare. Every cyclic Law has its own Unity
windows distributed across the year.

### Key-Derivative Unity

A Solar Key is active AND the LGBF vessel produces the same quality as
the Key. Example: it is sunrise (Gold Key = Fall of Events) AND the LGBF
has just opened Yin Qiao Mai (also Fall of Events). The Water gate and
the body's vessel carry the same quality. This is rare — it requires the
correct vessel to be open during the brief cusping window.

### Key-Amplified Unity

Two-Body Unity is active (Primeval and Derivative Laws match) AND a
Solar Key is simultaneously cusping. The Water gate carries its own
quality (Fall of Events at sunrise, Divinity at sunset) — different from
the unified Law — but its presence amplifies the moment. Two independent
systems agree on one Law while a third independent event (the solar
cusp) opens a Water gate, adding its own charge. This is the
instrument's highest-complexity structural compound, occurring at most a
few times per Divine Year.

### Why There Is No Three-Body Unity

A natural question: can the Primeval Law, the Derivative Law, AND a
Solar Key all carry the same quality at once? No. The 6+2 Architecture
makes this structurally impossible. The six cyclic Primeval Laws
(Kaos through Arrow of Complexity) are carried by six trigrams through
the lunar month. The two Water gate qualities (Fall of Events and
Divinity) are carried by Kǎn ☵ and Lí ☲ — the trigrams *excluded*
from the lunar cycle. The Primeval Law can never be Fall of Events or
Divinity; the Water gate can never carry a cyclic Law. Therefore
Primeval can never equal Key, and triple unity is impossible by design.
The Periodic Table (v5.0, Section 5) documents all six structural
impossibilities.

### What Unity Means

Law Unity does not make the moment "better" or "more powerful" in some
generic sense. It means the moment has an unusually coherent quality.
When multiple independent temporal systems say the same thing, the
alchemical character of that moment is concentrated rather than diffuse.
A practitioner who recognizes Unity can choose to work with it — or
simply notice it.

## Anatomical Intersection

A special case: when the LGBF vessel's confluent or coupled acupuncture
point falls on the meridian that is currently active in the Organ Clock.
Example: the LGBF opens Ren Mai (confluent point LU-7, on the Lung
meridian) during the Lung's active window. The body's deep reservoir
and its surface meridian are connected through the same access point
at the same time.

This is not Unity (which compares Laws). It is a different kind of
alignment — anatomical rather than cosmological. The two types can
co-occur: **Full Alignment** means Two-Body Unity AND Anatomical
Intersection at the same moment — the Laws agree AND the body's
channels are physically connected. Full Alignment is the rarest
structural compound, requiring three independent conditions to align
simultaneously.

**Dead zones.** Four Earthly Branches (Liver, Large Intestine, Stomach,
Heart) host no vessel confluent or coupled points at all. When the
Organ Clock enters one of these branches, Anatomical Intersection is
structurally impossible regardless of which vessel is open. The
Periodic Table documents the complete anatomical matrix.

## Cross-Dimensional Resonances

Beyond structural compounds (which compare Laws, Keys, and acupuncture
points), the Astrolabium detects **cross-dimensional resonances** —
moments when different interpretive dimensions of the state happen to
align. There are thirteen resonance types in four categories.

### Elemental Resonances — Wu Xing Matching

The Plum Blossom bridge (described above) gives every trigram a Wu Xing
element through the vessel-meridian chain. Each active organ also
carries a Wu Xing element. When these match, the cosmic principle and
the body's active channel share the same elemental character — read
through the body's own anatomy.

Three distinct Wu Xing resonances are tracked:

**Plum Blossom Match.** The Primeval trigram's Plum Blossom element
matches the active organ's element. The Plum Blossom mapping passes
through the Extraordinary Vessels — the same vessels the Astral layer
computes — threading all three temporal bodies through a single chain.
It distinguishes Imperial Fire, Ministerial Fire, Yin Water, and Yang
Water, creating a finer-grained elemental comparison than the canonical
five-element scheme.

**Vessel-Organ Match.** The Derivative vessel's own Plum Blossom element
matches the active organ's element. This reads the Astral-to-Gross
connection directly: the deep reservoir and the surface meridian share
the same Wu Xing nature.

**Canonical Trigram Match.** The Primeval trigram's traditional Wu Xing
(from the Later Heaven / King Wen arrangement: Qián = Metal, etc.)
matches the active organ's element. This is a positional-seasonal mapping
from the cosmological tradition. It sometimes confirms the Plum Blossom
resonance, sometimes diverges from it. When both agree, the elemental
alignment is confirmed through two independent derivations.

### Qualitative Resonances — Interpretive Alignment

These resonances compare *qualities* across systems rather than
mechanical timing values. They are graded EXACT, NATURAL, or NONE,
because the comparison involves human-judged semantic relationships.

**Color Affinity.** The Primeval Law's color matches the Chia color for
the active organ's Wu Xing element. Sole Atom (Green) during a Wood
organ (Green) = EXACT. Kaos (Yellow Gold) during an Earth organ
(Yellow) = NATURAL. The color mapping exists in the resonance lookup
tables; the Astrolabium does not invent affinities at runtime.

**Emotion-Perception Resonance.** The Chia emotional quality (positive
or negative) for the active organ's element semantically matches the
Primeval Law's perception pair. Divinity's perception pair includes
"Hatred" (negative); Fire's Chia emotion includes "Hatred" (negative)
= EXACT match. Time Matrix's perception includes "Pain/Suffering";
Metal's Chia emotion includes "Grief" = NATURAL affinity. These are
pre-authored semantic judgments, not runtime guesses.

**Adonaj-Ba Proximity.** The Primeval Law's Adonaj-Ba energy center
has an anatomical relationship with the currently active organ.
Synchronicity's Adonaj-Ba is the Heart; during the Heart meridian
window = EXACT. Kaos → Solar Plexus during Stomach = EXACT.
Relationships are graded: EXACT (same anatomical region), ADJACENT
(neighboring region), PROXIMAL (related via meridian pathway), or
NONE.

### Rhythmic Resonances — Temporal Phase Alignment

**Waxing-Wing Alignment.** The Moon's phase direction matches the solar
wing. Waxing Moon + Day Wing = aligned. Waning Moon + Night Wing =
aligned. The most frequently active resonance (~50% of all moments),
reflecting the natural harmony of lunar and solar rhythms.

**Yang Count.** Not a binary resonance but a tracked state value: the
number of yang lines in the current phase's trigram (0 at New Moon, up
to 3 at Full Moon, back down to 0). This traces the energetic wave
through the lunar month — a quantitative measure of the moment's
yang/yin character.

### Calendrical Resonances — Calendar-Layer Alignment

**Sephirotic-Quest Match.** The Sephirotic day's planet matches the
Primeval Law's Quest Tarot Continental attribution. Example: a Luna
day (Day 2) while Geometric Essence is the Primeval Law (Quest =
Priestess = Beth = Moon in Lévi's system) = match. This connects the
calendar's micro-rhythm (the Sephirotic week) with the Soul layer's
macro-rhythm (the lunar Law) through the Continental Tarot's
planetary attributions.

**Season-Great Rite Alignment.** The Chia season for the active
organ's element matches the current month's Great Rite seasonal
position. Wood organs (Spring) during the Spring Equinox month
(OSIRIS) = match. The body's seasonal character (from TCM) aligns
with the calendar's ritual character (from Damanhurian tradition).

Three additional calendrical **state values** are tracked — not binary
resonances but descriptors of the calendar's current position:

- **Alchemical Stage** — Nigredo (months 1–4), Albedo (5–8), Rubedo
  (9–12), or Da'ath (month 13, VADUSFADAHM)
- **Month Group** — Osirian, Falcon, Operative, or Tappetino
- **IAO Position** — The current month's position in the IAO cycle:
  I (ISIS), O (OSIRIS), A (SET), or null

### Resonance Grades

The nine Boolean resonances (those that are either active or inactive)

The Organ Clock and the Earthly Branch system are the SAME calculation.

is not coincidence — it is the natural consequence of shared foundations.

This is why civil clock time is never used anywhere in the system.
Every temporal layer derives from the Sun's actual position relative to
the horizon at the practitioner's specific location. An Astrolabium
reading at Damanhur and an Astrolabium reading at Los Angeles for the
"same moment" in UTC will show different hourly states because the Sun
is in a different position at each location.

## A Note on Computation

Everything the Astrolabium displays can be computed by hand. The
Guidebook (v2.2) contains every lookup table and every formula needed
to do so. A practitioner with a pen, an ephemeris, and a sunrise/sunset
table can compute the complete state of any moment in approximately
eleven minutes. The Guidebook Section 13 extends this to resonance
detection — an additional two to three minutes per query.

The software implementation is a convenience, not a requirement. The
instrument was designed so that the knowledge it encodes cannot be lost
to a server failure, a power outage, or a subscription expiration. The
tables exist on paper. The formulas are arithmetic. The astronomy is
in the sky.

## Glossary

**Adonaj-Ba** — The Damanhurian term for the body's energy centers
(comparable to but distinct from chakras). Each of the eight Laws
corresponds to a specific Adonaj-Ba.

**Alchemical Stage** — The current movement within the Divine Year:
Nigredo (months 1–4, the dark work), Albedo (5–8, the whitening),

**Confluent point** — The primary acupuncture point that opens an
Extraordinary Vessel. Each vessel has one confluent point.

**Compound** — A structural timing alignment where two or more
deterministic conditions are simultaneously satisfied. Eighteen
compound types are tracked: Law Unity variants, Anatomical
Intersection variants, and their combinations.

**Coupled point** — The secondary acupuncture point paired with a
vessel's confluent point. Together, confluent and coupled points define
the vessel's access.

**Cusping window** — The period of civil twilight during which a Solar
Key is active. Varies by latitude and season (roughly 30–40 minutes at
Damanhur's latitude).

**Dead zone** — An Earthly Branch (Liver, Large Intestine, Stomach,
Heart) where no vessel confluent or coupled points exist. Anatomical
Intersection is structurally impossible during these windows.

**Derivative Law** — A Primeval Law operating within Form, through the
Extraordinary Vessel system. The "Later Heaven" expression of the Law.

**Divine Hour** — One of eight unequal divisions of the day-night cycle.
Four waking hours (First Wing, I–IV) and four dream hours (Second Wing,
V–VIII). Derived from solar position.

**Earthly Branch** — One of the twelve divisions of the Chinese
sexagenary cycle. Used for hours (solar position) and days (date).

**Extraordinary Vessel** — One of eight deep energetic reservoirs in
Traditional Chinese Medicine, distinct from the twelve primary meridians.

**Full Alignment** — Two-Body Law Unity AND Anatomical Intersection
occurring simultaneously. The Laws agree AND the vessel's access point
falls on the active meridian.

**Gold Key** — The sunrise Water gate. Carries Kǎn ☵ (Fall of Events,
Yin Water). Named for true lead (真鉛) in Neidan tradition.

**Great Rite** — One of six major Damanhurian ceremonial events anchored
to the solar year: Autumn Equinox, Day of the Dead, Winter Solstice,
Spring Equinox, Divine Marriage, Summer Solstice.

**Heavenly Stem** — One of the ten elements in the Chinese sexagenary
cycle. Used for days (date) and hours (Five Rat Rule).

**IAO Cycle** — The three-letter mystical formula (Isis, Apophis, Osiris)
mapped across the Divine Year through the three months that bear those
names.

**Key-Amplified Unity** — Two-Body Law Unity active during a Solar Key
cusping window. The Key carries its own (different) Law, amplifying the
moment's complexity.

**Law Unity** — The condition where two or more temporal layers produce
the same Primeval Law simultaneously.

**LGBF** — Ling Gui Ba Fa (靈龜八法). The chronoacupuncture calculation
that determines which Extraordinary Vessel is currently open.

**Primeval Law** — One of eight cosmological principles governing the
universe of Form, as taught by Falco Tarassaco. The Soul layer of the
Astrolabium.

**Recipe** — A specific combination of simultaneously active compounds
and resonances. 351 unique recipes were observed across Divine Year 77.

**Resonance** — A cross-dimensional alignment where interpretive
qualities (element, color, emotion, anatomical proximity, calendar
position) match across layers. Thirteen resonance types in four
categories: elemental, qualitative, rhythmic, calendrical.

**Sephirotic Week** — The variable-length week (7–9 days) between
lunar quarter-points, with each day named for a planet and a quality.

**Plum Blossom Alchemy** — The elemental bridge between the 8-trigram

Derived from the Cantong qi tradition. This creates the system's

**Water Gate** — The Solar Key cusping moment. Both Key trigrams (Kǎn

**Unequal hours** — Time divisions derived from actual sunrise and
sunset, varying by season and latitude. All Astrolabium hourly
calculations use unequal hours. Never civil clock time.

**VADUSFADAHM** — The intercalary 13th month, inserted when the lunar
calendar drifts. The Activation. Corresponds to Da'ath (Knowledge /
the Abyss) in the Sephirotic system.
